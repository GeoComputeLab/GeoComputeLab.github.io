**Pengyu Chen** is a Ph.D. student in Geospatial and Environmental Analysis at Virginia Tech. He holds an M.S. in Geography from the University of South Carolina and a B.S. in Geographic Information Science from Wuhan University of Technology. His research integrates deep learning, spatial statistics, and computer vision for urban sensing, environmental monitoring, and planning.
